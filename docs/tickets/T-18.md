# T-18 · Google connect and push

**Model:** A (Opus, no implementer dispatch) · **Depends on:** T-17 · **Sections:** 10.2, 10.3, 9.6 (Integrations)

## What was built

- **`src/server/crypto.ts`:**
  - Tokens are encrypted with AES-256-GCM under `ENCRYPTION_KEY` (base64 of iv | tag | ciphertext).
  - The OAuth `state` is signed with HMAC-SHA256 under `AUTH_SECRET`. It holds the user, the space and a nonce, and
    expires after 10 minutes.
- **`src/server/google/api.ts`** is the only module that calls Google (googleapis 148): the consent URL, the code
  exchange (email from a verified id_token), refresh, revoke, and insert/patch/delete of events. Services call it
  through the `googleApi` object, so tests replace its methods and never touch the network.
- **`src/server/services/google-calendar.ts`:**
  - `getGoogleConnection`, `getGoogleAuthUrl`, `handleGoogleCallback` and `disconnectGoogle`.
  - `handleGoogleCallback` refuses a consent without the calendar scope, keeps an existing refresh token if Google
    omits one, and pushes the user's pending future blocks once connected.
  - Access tokens are refreshed when they expire within 60 s. `invalid_grant` deletes the connection and surfaces
    "Reconnect it in Integrations".
  - Push functions: `pushTimeBlock` (insert, or patch; a 404/410 recreates the event), `removeGoogleEvent`
    (404/410 = done), `pushTaskRename`, `unpushTasks` and `pushTasks`.
- **Push runs after the commit** (10.3), awaited inline, so the response already carries SYNCED or ERROR:
  - creating, moving or resizing a block pushes it; removing it deletes the event;
  - `updateTask` with a new title patches the task's future events;
  - `deleteTask` deletes the subtree's future events and marks those blocks PENDING; `restoreTask` pushes them back.
- **Retry:** `retrySync` service + action, plus a "Retry sync" item in the block ⋯ menu and a Retry button on the
  dialog's Scheduled row.
- **`/api/google/callback`:** verifies the session and state, stores the tokens, and redirects to
  `/s/<space>/integrations?google=connected|cancelled|error&message=…`.
- **UI:**
  - an Integrations page at `/s/[spaceId]/integrations`, with Connect, Disconnect (confirm) and outcome toasts;
  - "Integrations" in the user menu;
  - "Connect Google Calendar" in the calendar header when the server is configured and the user isn't connected;
  - sync icons, shown only when connected:
    - calendar blocks: a clock while PENDING, a warning while in ERROR (hover shows the error), nothing when SYNCED;
    - the dialog's Scheduled rows: ↻ synced, a clock while pending, a warning with Retry on error.
- **Env:** the app reads `GOOGLE_CLIENT_ID`, `GOOGLE_CLIENT_SECRET`, `APP_URL` and `ENCRYPTION_KEY`. The local
  `.env` had `CLIENT_ID` / `CLIENT_SECRET`; they were renamed.

## Checks

- **Tests:** 12 new tests (217 total): 4 crypto and 8 Google service, with Google faked.
  - Connect: encrypted storage, and pushing pending blocks on connect; a forged state or a missing scope is refused;
    disconnect revokes.
  - Push: create, patch and delete; a 404 recreates the event; a 429 gives ERROR, and Retry then gives SYNCED.
  - Tokens: refresh within 60 s; `invalid_grant` disconnects.
  - Renames, deletes and restores follow the task.
- **Browser** (Ada, Playwright + Chrome):
  - The sign-in page shows "Continue with Google".
  - The user menu has Integrations, and its card shows Connect.
  - Connect redirects to `accounts.google.com/o/oauth2/v2/auth` with `redirect_uri=http://localhost:3000/api/google/callback`,
    `scope=openid email …/auth/calendar.events`, `access_type=offline`, `prompt=consent`,
    `include_granted_scopes=true` and a state.
  - The callback: `access_denied` gives "wasn't connected"; a forged state gives "invalid or has expired"; a
    fake code gives "failed. Try again". Each one lands back on Integrations.
  - The calendar header links to Integrations.
- **Not yet verified:** a real consent with a Google account. That needs the human's account (seed users have fake
  addresses).
